Mochi Fast Privacy Policy

Effective October 23, 2026

Mochi Fast is a free fasting timer for Android, made by Nick of Time Canada. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the app. The short version: your fasts never leave your phone. The app shows a small banner ad from Google AdMob, which you can remove with a one-time purchase.

What the app stores

The app keeps the following only on your phone, in local app storage:

  • Your fasts: start and end times, the goal you chose, and whether you reached it.
  • Your settings: streak rhythm, theme, chosen Mochi flavor, notification and motion preferences.
  • Whether you have bought "Remove ads", so the banner stays hidden.

This information never leaves your device. There are no accounts, no cloud sync and no server of ours that receives it. Android backup is turned off for the app, so it is not copied to your Google account either.

Ads

Mochi Fast shows one small banner ad at the bottom of the screen, served by Google AdMob. We only request non-personalized ads. To show and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google's ad SDK may collect device identifiers (such as the advertising ID), approximate location derived from your IP address, app interaction data and diagnostic information. This data goes to Google, not to us, and is handled under Google's advertising policies and Google's Privacy Policy. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings.

Your fasting data is never shared with Google or any ad network.

What the app does not do

  • No analytics or tracking SDKs of our own, and no full-screen ads.
  • No access to your contacts, photos, health data or other apps' data.
  • No selling of your data.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, reminders about fasting stages and goals are scheduled locally on your phone. No push service is used and nothing is sent to us.

Remove ads purchase

The optional "Remove ads" purchase uses Google Play Billing. Google processes the payment under Google's Privacy Policy. We do not receive your payment details.

Deleting your data

You can delete individual fasts in the app's History. Uninstalling Mochi Fast removes all of its data from your phone. We hold no data about you on any server.

Children

Mochi Fast is intended for adults. It is not directed at children under 18, and the app reminds people under 18 to talk to a doctor before fasting.

Not medical advice

Mochi Fast is a wellness timer, not a medical device, and nothing in it is medical advice.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
