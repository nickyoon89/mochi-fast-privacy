Mochi Fast Privacy Policy

Effective October 23, 2026

Mochi Fast is a free fasting timer for Android, made by Nick of Time Canada. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the app. The short version: Mochi Fast does not collect, store on a server, or share any of your personal data.

What the app stores

The app keeps the following only on your phone, in local app storage:

  • Your fasts: start and end times, the goal you chose, and whether you reached it.
  • Your settings: streak rhythm, theme, chosen Mochi flavor, notification and motion preferences.
  • Whether you have left a tip, so the app can say thank you.

This information never leaves your device. There are no accounts, no cloud sync and no server of ours that receives it. Android backup is turned off for the app, so it is not copied to your Google account either.

What the app does not do

  • No analytics, tracking or advertising SDKs.
  • No ads.
  • No access to your location, contacts, photos, health data or other apps' data.
  • No selling or sharing of data with anyone.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, reminders about fasting stages and goals are scheduled locally on your phone. No push service is used and nothing is sent to us.

Optional tips

The tip jar uses Google Play Billing. If you choose to tip, Google processes the payment under Google's Privacy Policy. We do not receive your payment details. Tips are optional and do not unlock anything.

Deleting your data

You can delete individual fasts in the app's History. Uninstalling Mochi Fast removes all of its data from your phone. Because we hold no data about you, there is nothing to delete on our side.

Children

Mochi Fast is intended for adults. It is not directed at children under 18, and the app reminds people under 18 to talk to a doctor before fasting.

Not medical advice

Mochi Fast is a wellness timer, not a medical device, and nothing in it is medical advice.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
